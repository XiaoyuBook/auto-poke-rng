# BDSP target sprites

These 47 PNG files are the National Pokédex ID sprites used by the automatic
target preview. They were downloaded from the [PokéAPI sprites repository](https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites/tree/master/sprites/pokemon/versions/generation-viii/brilliant-diamond-shining-pearl)
on 2026-09-26. The filenames match `speciesId` in `src/generated/bdsp-data.json`.

PokéAPI's [license notice](https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites/blob/master/LICENCE.txt)
states that the image contents are copyright The Pokémon Company.
